physics = phy si \[ks\] = physix = phy6

宇宙是虚拟的，即存在一个最小时间间隔$\Delta t$

证明：
$$
\Delta t^2 = \frac{d^2s\Delta t^2}{d^2s} = \frac{\mathrm{d}t^2\times d\Delta s}{\mathrm{d}^2s} = \frac{d\Delta s}{a} 
$$

$$
\frac{d\Delta s}{a}=  \frac{s \times d\Delta m}{F} = \frac{d\Delta m}{p} = \frac{d\Delta m}{P} = \frac{dm \Delta t}{W}
$$

$$
\frac{dm \Delta t}{W} = \frac{dm \Delta t}{Q_吸} = \frac{d}{c} = \frac{d}{C} = \frac{1}{\pi}
$$

$$
\Delta t = \frac{1}{\sqrt \pi}
$$
